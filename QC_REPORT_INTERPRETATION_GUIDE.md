# Interpreting & Deciphering `resipsc` QC Reports

This guide provides a comprehensive breakdown for understanding, interpreting, and acting upon the results generated by the **resipsc v2.0** Quality Control (QC) pipeline after uploading or selecting a single-cell RNA sequencing dataset (`.h5ad`, `.csv`, `.mtx`).

---

## 1. The Core Verdict: The 3 Release Decisions

At the top of the Elevated QC Report, the system displays a prominent decision banner representing the automated disposition of the analyzed cell therapy batch:

| Decision | Badge Color | Meaning & Criteria | Regulatory & Clinical Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RELEASE** | Green | **Batch Pass:** Residual PSC fraction is below the detection floor ($\le 0.001\%$). Zero high-confidence stem cells detected. | Eligible for automated release into the clinical or downstream manufacturing pipeline. |
| **HOLD** | Amber | **Borderline Risk:** Estimated residual PSC fraction falls between $0.001\%$ and $1.0\%$, or trace borderline cells are flagged. | Automated release paused. Requires Senior QA / Qualified Person (QP) review and orthogonal testing (e.g., ddPCR, flow cytometry). |
| **QUARANTINE** | Red | **Batch Failed:** Residual PSC contamination $> 1.0\%$ or a contiguous cluster of undifferentiated cells is present. | Batch rejected and quarantined to prevent potential teratoma (tumor) risk in patients. |

---

## 2. Executive Summary Metrics (The 4 Key Numbers)

Immediately below the disposition banner, four primary analytical tiles summarize the batch purity:

```
┌─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
│ Estimated PSC Fraction  │  Cells Above Threshold  │ Biomarker-Model Agreem. │    Dominant Lineage     │
│         0.2879%         │       1 / 500 cells     │          98.4%          │        Endoderm         │
└─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Estimated PSC Fraction (e.g., `0.2879%`)**:
   * **What it means:** The model’s calibrated estimate of the percentage of cells in the batch that are undifferentiated, residual pluripotent stem cells.
   * **Methodology:** The raw cell count crossing the probability cutoff is adjusted by an empirical calibration factor ($S_e = 69.47\%$) derived from multi-site spike-in sweeps to correct for technical single-cell dropouts.
2. **Cells Above Threshold (e.g., `1 / 500 cells`)**:
   * **What it means:** The absolute count of individual cells whose predicted PSC probability exceeded the decision threshold ($\theta \approx 0.50$).
3. **Biomarker-Model Agreement (e.g., `98.4%`)**:
   * **What it means:** Concordance between the non-linear Machine Learning ensemble (XGBoost + RF + SVM + Deep MLP) and established biological biomarker panels (*POU5F1 / OCT4, SOX2, NANOG*).
   * **Interpretation:** Scores $> 90\%$ confirm that computational predictions match underlying biological ground truth. If agreement drops significantly, inspect for technical noise or ambient RNA broth.
4. **Dominant Lineage (e.g., `Endoderm`, `Ectoderm`, or `Mesoderm`)**:
   * **What it means:** Identifies the primary germ layer identity of the mature, differentiated population (e.g., *Endoderm* for hepatocyte/pancreatic lineages, *Ectoderm* for dopaminergic neurons/RPE, *Mesoderm* for cardiomyocytes).

## 3. Regulatory Risk Tiers (T1 to T4)

`resipsc` categorizes batches according to FDA CBER and EMA cell therapy purity standards:

* **Tier 1 (T1 - Ultra-Low Risk):** $< 0.01\%$ residual PSCs $\to$ Standard release candidate.
* **Tier 2 (T2 - Low Risk):** $0.01\% - 0.1\%$ residual PSCs $\to$ Acceptable for research; requires secondary confirmation for clinical lots.
* **Tier 3 (T3 - Moderate Risk):** $0.1\% - 1.0\%$ residual PSCs $\to$ Automatic **HOLD** disposition; QA investigation mandatory.
* **Tier 4 (T4 - High Risk):** $> 1.0\%$ residual PSCs $\to$ Automatic **QUARANTINE / REJECT** disposition.

## 4. Deep-Dive Analytical Sections

### A. Statistical Confidence
* **Bootstrap 95% Confidence Interval (1,000 iterations):**
  Provides a statistical credible interval around the estimated contamination (e.g., `[0.00%, 1.08%]`). If the upper bound of the interval sits safely below regulatory thresholds, statistical certainty is high.
* **Binomial Test ($p$-value):**
  Calculates the probability that the observed contamination rate occurred purely by random chance against the null hypothesis ($H_0: \text{fraction} \le 0.001\%$). A $p$-value $< 0.05$ confirms statistically significant contamination.

### B. Lineage Breakdown & Teratoma Hazard
* Breaks down the cellular composition into mature lineages vs. pluripotent cells.
* Undifferentiated stem cells with multi-lineage differentiation potential carry a significantly higher teratoma hazard index than committed intermediate precursors.

### C. Gene Expression Heatmap & Biomarker Profiling
* Visualizes expression intensities of core pluripotency master regulators:
  * `POU5F1` (*OCT4*), `SOX2`, `NANOG`
  * Secondary markers: `LIN28A`, `ZFP42` (*REX1*), `EPCAM`, `PODXL`
* Helps verify whether flagged single cells exhibit genuine transcriptional activation of the pluripotency network or if high counts are driven by ambient transcript leakage.

### D. 21 CFR Part 11 Audit Trail & Data Integrity
* Logs every analysis into an append-only, SHA-256 chained SQLite ledger (`db/audit.db`).
* Displays cryptographic hashes for the input file, runtime parameters, and resulting JSON payload to ensure the report is immutable and tamper-evident.

1. **When Verdict is RELEASE:**
   * Click **Download 14-page PDF Report** to export the formal audit record for electronic archiving.
2. **When Verdict is HOLD:**
   * Review the **Recommendations** table in the dashboard.
   * Conduct targeted orthogonal assays (e.g., ddPCR for *LIN28A* or *ESRG*) on the specific flagged subpopulation.
3. **When Verdict is QUARANTINE:**
   * Check the **Per-Cell Classification** table and **Gene Heatmap** to diagnose where differentiation arrested.
   * Document batch rejection in accordance with standard operating procedures (SOPs).
