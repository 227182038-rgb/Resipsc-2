Trial Datasets Validation
Positive:
 real single-cell RNA-
seq
 containing pluripotent or undifferentiated cells.
Negative:
 real human tissue where no pluripotent cells are expected.
“Authentication”:
 I took this to mean published, peer-reviewed data with a traceable public accession.
Positive datasets (pluripotent or undifferentiated cells present)
P1. GSE75748: 
hESC
 to definitive endoderm
 (Chu et al., 
Genome Biology
 2016)
The paper reconstructs a trajectory from the pluripotent state through 
mesendoderm
 to definitive endoderm, covering 1,776 cells.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4989499
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-016-1033-x
https://omicsdi.org/dataset/biostudies-arrayexpress/E-GEOD-75748
P2. GSE248497: 
hPSC
 to limbal stem cells
 (
Vattulainen
 et al., 
Stem Cell Reports
 2024)
This is the strongest “residual cells” case. Three 
hPSC
 lines were differentiated, and the paper reports mesodermal-like and undifferentiated subpopulations whose prevalence depends on the line.
The accession comes from the paper’s data-availability statement.
https://www.cell.com/stem-cell-reports/fulltext/S2213-6711(24)00153-X
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.11.24.568553.full.pdf
P3. CNP0001218: H9 
hESC
 to limbal stem cells, time course
 (Sun et al., 
Cell & Bioscience
 2021)
Day 0 has 4,687 cells; the paper reports POU5F1 in 99.98% of them, so this is a clean pluripotent reference.
The data are on 
CNGBdb
 (China National 
GeneBank
), not GEO. Access terms may differ, so check them.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7824938/
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13578-021-00541-4
P4. E-MTAB-6268: 
hPSC
 cardiac differentiation
 (Friedman et al., 
Cell Stem Cell
 2018)
ArrayExpress
 accession, confirmed on the publication record.
https://cmdga.org/publications/01d693b8-7142-435c-b58f-9c8d31abb001/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6220122
